Our History & Legacy

Building strong foundations across generations.

Established in 1988

Neil Construction Company was originally founded in 1988 by Eric Bjorn Neilson, establishing a reputation for craftsmanship, precision, and high-quality construction.

In 2012, the company entered a new era when it was reopened by his son, Johan Sven Neilson, who holds degrees in Architecture and Civil Engineering from Stockholm University. Under his leadership, the business expanded into Rivergate, Cape Town, and international project delivery.

Following a temporary closure in 2021 due to COVID-19 complications, Neil Construction Company restructured its global operations to better serve residential, commercial, and engineering developments across Africa, North America, and Europe.
